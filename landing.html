our mission

We want to change the way people learn and view mistakes. Making a mistake has been drilled into our brain as something worth punishing. Konfide is here to change that.

Making a mistake shouldn't limit you by getting less XP, loosing hearts or limiting the time you spend on learning. Trying to master a language takes a lot of trial and error, and it can be very frustrating to be punished for every little thing. We focused on making this journey a much more kind and enjoyable.

Imagine you're out with your friends, you're laughing and enjoying the day. But suddenly, it starts raining. And instead of being upset and going home and feeling sad, you start dancing and playing in the rain. You get absolutely drenched, but it was so worth it.

This is how learning with Konfide feels. It turns a normally infuriating experience into something fun and memorable.
